# Loggyn hero background generation prompts

All four assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow in `photorealistic-natural` mode and then exported as optimized project-local JPEGs.

## Purple rose wall

Final asset: `public/images/hero-roses-purple.jpg`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: premium 16:9 website hero background for a modern women’s health and gynecology clinic
Primary request: Create a luxurious, continuous wall of tightly packed roses in rich purple, plum, mulberry, deep mauve and muted orchid tones, visually related to the supplied former hero background but clearly a new composition. The rose wall should feel abundant and tactile rather than like a loose bouquet.
Input images: Image 1 is a visual reference for the sophisticated dark plum palette, cinematic softness, and premium mood only; do not copy its exact arrangement.
Scene/backdrop: Roses fill the entire frame as a cohesive floral wall, with no table, vase, stems, room, horizon, or visible background surface.
Subject: Many medium and small layered garden roses and ranunculus-like blooms, overlapping naturally with subtle depth, realistic petal variation, a few buds, and restrained shadow gaps.
Style/medium: High-end editorial floral photography, photorealistic, finely detailed natural petals, refined and contemporary, not synthetic.
Composition/framing: Wide landscape 16:9. Preserve generous usable copy space across the left 45% by making that region darker, lower contrast and visually calmer while still visibly composed of roses; progressively reveal richer floral detail and slightly brighter mauve highlights toward the right. Avoid a hard split or an empty flat gradient—the floral wall must remain continuous across the whole image. Crop safely for responsive website cover behavior.
Lighting/mood: Soft directional studio light from the upper right, deep cinematic plum shadows, intimate, calm, confident and premium.
Color palette: Aubergine, blackberry, burgundy-purple, dusty mauve, muted orchid and selective pale lilac highlights; clearly more purple than pink, with no bright red, orange, yellow, green or blue.
Materials/textures: Velvety realistic petals, subtle natural imperfections, soft tonal depth.
Constraints: No text, no logo, no lettering, no watermark, no people, no medical objects. No empty tabletop or artificial flat color field. The left side must support ivory website copy with strong readability. Keep the image elegant and not gloomy.
Avoid: giant isolated hero flowers, sparse bouquet arrangement, obvious repeating pattern, plastic petals, neon magenta, oversaturation, harsh specular shine, greenery, decorative props, borders, frames.
```

## Straight-on open rose wall

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: premium 16:9 website hero background for Loggyn, a modern private gynecology clinic
Primary request: Create a straight-on rose-wall composition in purple and plum tones, with fewer flowers visible than the supplied dense rose-wall references and generous calm empty space in the top-left.
Input images: Image 1 is a reference for a continuous rose-wall idea and small-flower texture. Image 2 is a reference for the desired purple/plum palette. Create an original composition rather than copying either arrangement.
Scene/backdrop: a matte deep-aubergine wall viewed perfectly from the front. A curated floral installation of medium-size roses is mounted flush to the wall, mainly across the right side and lower edge.
Subject: realistic layered roses and a few buds in dusty mauve, muted orchid, blackberry, burgundy-purple and pale lilac. Use noticeably fewer individual flowers than a dense tapestry wall, leaving breathing room and clearly readable individual blooms.
Style/medium: high-end editorial botanical photography, photorealistic, premium and medically calm.
Composition/framing: wide horizontal 16:9. Preserve the upper-left 45% as a smooth, dark, low-detail negative-space field for large white copy. Let the flower wall begin softly around the center-right and become fuller toward the right and lower edge. Keep the arrangement visually balanced and gently organic, not a hard rectangular block.
Lighting/mood: soft frontal studio light with subtle depth in petals, calm and sophisticated, restrained contrast.
Color palette: aubergine, plum, blackberry, dusty mauve, muted purple, selective pale lilac; more purple than pink; no red, orange, yellow, green or blue cast.
Constraints: fewer roses, front-on camera, visible dark wall at upper-left, flowers stay clear of the copy-safe area, no people, no objects, no text, no logo, no watermark.
Avoid: angled camera, diagonal composition, macro close-up, oversized cropped flowers, full-frame dense flower carpet, bouquet, vase, stems crossing the empty space, greenery, wedding styling, plastic petals, neon-magenta oversaturation.
```
